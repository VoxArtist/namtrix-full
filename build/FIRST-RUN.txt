NAMTRIX Full — read this once
=============================

1. Drag "NAMTRIX Full.app" to your Applications folder.

2. The first time, RIGHT-CLICK it and choose "Open" — do not double-click.
   macOS will ask whether you are sure. Say yes.

   A plain double-click will say the developer cannot be verified and refuse.
   That is not a fault in the app: telling macOS otherwise requires a paid
   Apple Developer account. Right-click → Open once and it remembers forever.

3. macOS will ask for microphone permission. Say yes.

   It counts any audio input as a microphone, including your interface. If you
   say no, recordings come back silent instead of giving you an error, which is
   a confusing way to lose an hour. You can change it later in
   System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

That is the whole installation. Nothing else to install — the app carries its
own Python and audio libraries.

Double-click it and your browser opens on the tool. Quit from the Dock when you
are done.

If you extract the zip from a terminal, use "ditto -x -k" rather than "unzip":
unzip does not preserve everything the app's signature covers, and macOS will
then refuse to run it.
